About Ethan Mokoena

From Bryanston High to representing South Africa on the world stage

From Bryanston to the World

Ethan Mokoena is a Grade 11 learner at Bryanston High School, where he has established himself as one of the most promising dual-sport athletes in the country. Excelling in both track and rugby, Ethan has caught the attention of national selectors.

Track Career

Ethan specializes in the 400m and 4x400m relay. His personal best of 10.8 seconds in the 100m demonstrates his raw speed, but it's his endurance and tactical awareness that make him a standout in the 400m. He has represented Gauteng at the national level twice.

Rugby Career

As a flanker for the Bryanston 1st XV, Ethan combines his speed with physicality. His ability to read the game and make crucial tackles has made him a key player for his school team. Coaches describe him as "a once-in-a-generation athlete."

The Big Opportunity

In November 2026, Ethan will represent South Africa at the Commonwealth Youth Games. This is a dream come true, but it comes with significant costs:

  • International flights
  • Accommodation
  • Competition kit
  • Training camps
  • Medical support

Ethan is not just a talented athlete; he's a leader, a role model, and a student who balances his sports with academics. He's the kind of young South African we should all be proud to support.

— Coach Mark Peters, Bryanston High
